Sentinel Zero Services

Sentinel Zero provides cybersecurity and business systems services within an agreed client scope. This page describes services; it does not itself run calling, CRM, or notification systems.

Core Services

Cybersecurity Risk Assessments

Full review of your business systems to identify vulnerabilities.

Device & Network Security Review

Evaluation of phones, computers, and networks for potential exposure.

Operational Risk Analysis

Identifying weak processes that could lead to financial or data loss.

Security Advisory

Clear recommendations on how to fix and secure your setup.

Advanced Capabilities (SSAG Integration)

AI Outbound Appointment Setting

AI-assisted outreach and appointment workflows are available only after a supported calling provider is connected, configured, and verified.

CRM & Pipeline Systems

Lead and opportunity tracking; shared records require a configured, secured backend.

Automation & Follow-Up Systems

Follow-up workflows help reduce missed leads; delivery depends on configured alerts, assigned owners, and working data connections.

Business Infrastructure Systems

Backend planning and build work scoped to the agreed integrations, access, and tested requirements.

Position

Sentinel Zero is not just a cybersecurity service — it is part of a larger system designed to protect, optimize, and scale business operations.
